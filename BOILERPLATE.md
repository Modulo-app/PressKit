# Modulo — Boilerplate

Use this paragraph as-is in articles or press coverage:

> Modulo is a personal finance app for iOS that helps people manage their money with the 50/30/20 budgeting method — splitting income across Needs, Wants, and Savings — while keeping subscriptions and recurring payments under control. Built by solo developer Théo Sementa, Modulo relies on simple, declarative entry with no bank account linking required.
