# Modulo — Fact Sheet

**Category:** Finance / Personal Budgeting

**App Store:** Soon

## Short description

Modulo helps you manage your money with the 50/30/20 budgeting method — split your income across Needs, Wants, and Savings, and keep your subscriptions and recurring payments under control.

## Long description

Modulo is a personal finance app built around the 50/30/20 budgeting method: 50% of your income for Needs, 30% for Wants, and 20% for Savings. Instead of passively logging purchases, Modulo helps you actively manage your budget across these three categories, with a dedicated view for tracking subscriptions and recurring payments.

Modulo uses manual, declarative entry rather than bank account linking, keeping the app simple and free of third-party financial data sharing.

## Key features

- Budget tracking using the 50/30/20 method (Needs / Wants / Savings)
- Subscription and recurring payment management
- Manual, declarative entry — no bank account linking required
- Monthly summaries
